# Iron & Oak Closets — System Architecture & Operational Playbook

## Executive Summary & Core Concept
* **High-Concept Pitch:** Modular, structural closet installations ranging from industrial-strength basic hanging systems to luxury, fully customized storage suites, paired with an optional full-service concierge wardrobe reset.
* **Core Innovation:** Standardized structural hardware across all tiers anchored by an ultra-heavy-duty wall-mounting system rated for 300+ lbs, enabling seamless client upgrades from Tier 1 to Tier 3 over time without wall damage.
* **Market Opportunity:** Bridges the gap between cheap builder-grade wire racks ($200–$500, prone to collapsing) and hyper-expensive custom millwork ($5,000–$15,000+ with long lead times).
* **Target ICP:** Suburban homeowners, first-time home buyers (ages 28–45), rental property managers, and luxury homeowners seeking turn-key organizational solutions.
* **Core Guarantee:** "Bulletproof weight capacity at every price point"—a lifetime anti-collapse warranty on all structural framing.

## Hardware Sourcing & Standardization Architecture

### Sourcing Strategy Matrix
* **Tier 1 (Heavy-Duty Iron Rod):** 
  * *Material:* 1-inch to 1.25-inch Schedule 40 black iron pipe or galvanized steel.
  * *Sourcing:* Direct commercial accounts with industrial distributors (e.g., Ferguson, McMaster-Carr) or regional steel service centers. Pre-cut/threaded in 10-foot lengths to eliminate job-site cutting.
  * *Anchoring:* Floor/wall flanges secured to studs via 1/4" x 2.5" structural lag screws.
* **Tier 2 (The Utility Stack):**
  * *Material:* Powder-coated steel hang rails, vertical standards, adjustable bracket arms, and structural composite shelving.
  * *Sourcing:* Commercial OEM storage suppliers (e.g., Schulte, ClosetMaid Professional, Knape & Vogt) at 40–50% off retail.
* **Tier 3 (The Master Suite):**
  * *Substrates:* 3/4" Thermally Fused Laminate (TFL) over industrial-grade MDF (80% of builds to eliminate on-site painting/finishing), Baltic Birch, or wood veneer for stained custom units.
  * *Sourcing:* 4'x8' sheets via regional architectural panel suppliers.

### Universal Mounting System (Upgrade Path)
* **Universal Top Rail:** 14-gauge cold-rolled steel continuous wall track mounted into every stud.
* **Tier 1 Attachment:** Steel pipe hangers lock directly into the universal top rail using quick-pin clevis fasteners.
* **Tier 2 Attachment:** Vertical notched standards snap directly onto the universal top rail.
* **Tier 3 Attachment:** Structural MDF side panels slot into the exact same top rail system, converting the system into a built-in suite without tearing out original wall attachments.

## Financial Models & Pricing Formulas

### Financial Target Metrics
* **Target Gross Margin:** Minimum **65%** overall across all tiers and services.
* **Target Labor Cost:** **15% – 18%** of total job price (sub-contracted or employee installer flat-rate payouts).

### Base Retail Pricing Formula
$$\text{Linear Foot Retail Price} = \frac{\text{Direct Material Cost/ft} + \text{Direct Labor Cost/ft}}{1 - \text{Target Gross Margin Percent}}$$

## Premium Accessories & Upgrades (Tier 3 Upsell)

* **Positioning:** High-margin, sales-floor upsell layered onto a Tier 3 Master Suite quote. Priced and sold as discrete line items, not baked into the linear-foot rate.
* **Sourcing Model:** Fulfilled through certified third-party safe/vault manufacturers (e.g., Liberty Safe, AMSEC) rather than in-house fabrication — Iron & Oak handles carpentry integration and install; the manufacturer's own warranty covers the unit itself.

### Liability & Compliance Protocol
* **Not Covered by Structural Warranty:** Safes and vaults carry the manufacturer's own warranty; Iron & Oak's Lifetime Structural Guarantee covers the surrounding closet framing only, not the safe mechanism, lock, or fire/burglary rating.
* **Gun Safe Compliance:** Installer confirms state/local child-access and storage law requirements with the client before install; safe must be bolted per manufacturer spec (floor and/or wall anchoring) as a condition of sale.
* **Sales Script Cue:** Position as *"We build the fortress around it — the safe itself comes from a certified manufacturer with its own warranty, so you get bank-grade security backed by a bank-grade brand."*

## White-Glove Reset Concierge Integration

### Package Breakdown
* **Basic Reset:** Systematically pack closet contents into mobile wardrobe boxes, complete hardware installation, and re-hang clothing systematically.
* **Concierge Reset:** Full inventorying, garment purging support, professional color-coded folding and hanging, inclusion of matching velvet/hardwood hangers (100-pack included), shoe/accessory styling, and old box removal.

### Two-Person Operational Protocol


[Arrival & Digital Pre-Audit] (Installer + Organizer)
│
▼
[Pack-Down & Wardrobe Staging] (Organizer Lead)
└── Mobile rolling wardrobe racks & stackable totes outside work zone
│
▼
[Tear-Out & Structural Hardware Build] (Installer Lead)
└── Remove old hardware, mount top track, assemble Tier 1-3 modules, cleanup
│
▼
[Garment Reload & Professional Restyling] (Organizer Lead)
└── Transfer to matching hangers, color block (Light → Dark), organize shoe/drawer galleries

### Risk & Liability Protocols
* **High-Value Waiver:** Client must self-remove all jewelry, cash, high-end watches, firearms, and luxury handbags prior to service.
* **Pre-Existing Damage Audit:** High-resolution digital photo log taken of all existing closet conditions and wardrobe items prior to touching contents.

## Digital Quote Engine & Lead Conversion Architecture

### Calculator Logic Workflow
1. **User Inputs:** Wall length (linear feet), closet shape (Reach-in vs. Walk-in), and current photos.
2. **Hardware Selection:** User toggles between Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 pricing.
3. **Service Add-On:** Dynamic checkbox for **Basic Reset** ($35/ft, min $350) or **Concierge Reset** ($75/ft, min $750).
4. **Formula Execution:** `Total Quote = (Selected Tier $/ft * Linear Ft) + MAX(Service Floor Price, Service $/ft * Linear Ft)`
5. **Output:** Instant 3D parametric preview + 3-tiered price matrix + $99 refundable deposit booking link.

## Lifetime Warranty Structure

* **Lifetime Structural Guarantee:** Covers wall anchoring integrity, steel pipe failure, standard track sagging, and structural joinery for homeownership duration.
* **5-Year Mechanical Warranty:** Covers soft-close drawer slides, hinges, and edge-banding.
* **Weight Limits:** 200 lbs/linear foot for Tier 1; 100 lbs/linear foot for Tiers 2 & 3.
